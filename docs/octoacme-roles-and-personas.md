# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality strategy, test planning, and validation of features. They collaborate with product and engineering to ensure acceptance criteria are met and quality standards are maintained before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test strategy and approach for each project
- Plan acceptance testing and identify test scenarios
- Lead manual QA efforts and coordinate automation testing
- Track and report quality metrics and defect status
- Coordinate with developers on test coverage and CI integration
- Validate readiness for release and production deployment

### Goals
- Reduce production defects and rework cycles
- Enable fast, confident deployments through comprehensive test coverage
- Provide clear visibility into quality status during execution

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and execution reviews with Developers and Project Managers
- Defect and quality reports in weekly syncs
- Release readiness reviews and post-deployment verification
- Risk identification during planning (e.g., tight timelines, complex features)

---

## Security Officer

### Role Summary
Security Officers embed security practices throughout the project lifecycle, from design to deployment. They ensure compliance, threat mitigation, and incident response readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Review designs for security risks and threats
- Audit code and infrastructure for vulnerabilities
- Coordinate security scanning in CI/CD pipelines
- Manage incident response and post-incident reviews
- Advise on compliance and regulatory requirements
- Communicate security status to stakeholders

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and data exposure
- Enable secure, compliant product delivery
- Foster a security-aware culture within the team

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and threat modeling sessions with Developers and Technical Leads
- Security scanning reports and remediation tracking
- Incident response escalation (see Risk Management guide)
- Quarterly security training and awareness updates

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define technical direction, review design decisions, and ensure code quality and system reliability. They mentor developers and help navigate complex technical trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Review technical proposals and pull requests for scalability and maintainability
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers and support skill development
- Ensure adherence to coding standards and best practices
- Participate in system design and capacity planning

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, well-documented systems
- Reduce technical debt and maintenance burden
- Enable team growth and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions with Developers and Product Managers
- Code review feedback and mentoring
- Sprint planning (technical risk and feasibility assessment)
- Engineering-level roadmap planning

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into clear requirements and acceptance criteria. They work closely with stakeholders and product managers to ensure solutions address actual business problems.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Translate business needs into technical requirements and user stories
- Define detailed acceptance criteria for features
- Validate solutions against business objectives
- Create process flows, workflows, and documentation for end users
- Serve as bridge between business stakeholders and the delivery team

### Goals
- Ensure solutions solve the actual business problem
- Reduce rework due to misaligned requirements
- Enable stakeholder confidence through clear, shared understanding

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and requirements workshops with Product Managers and Project Managers
- User story refinement and acceptance criteria review with Developers
- Release notes and user documentation
- Post-release feedback and lessons learned

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide strategic direction, resource allocation, and business context. They remove organizational blockers and ensure alignment with broader business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Allocate budget and resources
- Approve major decisions and trade-offs
- Remove organizational blockers and dependencies
- Receive regular status updates and escalations
- Approve release and go-live decisions

### Goals
- Maximize business value and ROI from projects
- Ensure alignment with strategic objectives
- Enable rapid, unblocked project delivery

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates with Project Managers and Product Managers (see Risk Management guide)
- Approval and decision reviews at key gates
- Escalation of major risks and blockers
- Release announcements and post-launch reviews
